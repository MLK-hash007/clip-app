# Clip - Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: January 2, 2025**

## Overview

Clip is a clipboard history manager designed with privacy as a core principle. Your data stays on your device.

## Data Collection

**We do not collect any personal data.**

Clip stores clipboard history locally on your device using Apple's Core Data framework. This data:
- Never leaves your device
- Is not transmitted to any server
- Is not shared with any third parties
- Is not used for analytics or advertising

## Data Stored Locally

The following data is stored only on your device:
- Clipboard text content you copy
- Timestamps of when items were copied
- Your pinned items preferences

## Data Retention

- OTP codes: Automatically deleted after 10 minutes
- URLs: Automatically deleted after 48 hours
- Plain text: Automatically deleted after 24 hours
- Pinned items: Kept until you delete them
- You can manually delete all data at any time in Settings

## Keyboard Extension

The Clip Keyboard extension requires "Full Access" to function. This permission is used solely to:
- Read clipboard content when the keyboard is active
- Insert text into text fields

Even with Full Access enabled, no data is transmitted off your device.

## Third-Party Services

Clip does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

## In-App Purchases

If you purchase Clip Pro, the transaction is handled entirely by Apple. We do not receive or store any payment information.

## Children's Privacy

Clip does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last Updated" date.
